[ THE PROBLEM ]

Patching an old site just hides the real problem.

Most sites that stop working weren't broken in one place. The information is in the wrong order, the proof is missing or buried, and there's no clear next step for a visitor to take. A new coat of paint on that structure just makes the wrong structure look nicer.

Starting over means deciding, before any design work happens, what a visitor needs to see, in what order, and what you want them to do next. Everything else (design, copy, code) gets built to support that decision, not the other way around.
