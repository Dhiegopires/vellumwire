[ THE PROBLEM ]

Someone is searching for you on Google right now.

Every day, people in your area type what you do into Google and Maps: "plumber near me," "dentist open now," "roofer emergency repair." Google decides who shows up first using a handful of concrete signals: complete and accurate information, real photos, recent activity, and how fast you respond to reviews.

Most small business listings were set up once, years ago, and never touched again. Hours are wrong. Photos are missing or outdated. Reviews sit unanswered for months. To Google, that reads as an inactive business, and it ranks accordingly. To a customer scanning results in ten seconds, it reads as "maybe closed" or "not sure I trust this one," and they call the next name on the list instead.
